TapESTORY

A storybook that paints itself, one tap at a time.

How to play
  • Tap anywhere on the picture (or press any key) exactly on the beat.
  • Listen for the cue sound - it shows you what's coming and when to respond.
  • A good tap paints the story in and pops a sticker. A missed beat shows what went wrong instead, but the story keeps going either way.
  • Keep your accuracy up across the whole song for the good ending.
  • First time playing? Run Calibrate once so tap timing matches your device - you can Recalibrate anytime from the menu.

The three corner marks on the picture:

  • Top-left circle - shows the glyph of whichever cue just sounded (its name pops up briefly the first time you hear it this session).
  • Top-right gold dot - a quiet metronome pulse, once per beat.
  • Bottom-right emoji - the game's mascot: it pulses on cues, cheers on a hit, shakes on a miss, and inflates during a hold.
